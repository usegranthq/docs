title: Security
---

Our authentication system is built on `zero trust principles`.
Your service implementations and use cases remain completely private with you, on your servers.

We only collect the essential information: your signup email to authenticate you to the app.
We do not collect or store any data about your services or intended usage patterns.
We log only the essential system logs (never any user data of any kind) to ensure the app is running as expected.
These logs are wiped after a short period of time (not exceeding 30 days).

This ensures maximum privacy and reduces the risk of sensitive information being exposed.
By limiting the data we store, we minimize the potential impact of any data breaches.

### Breach Resistant

These measures make our system breach resistant.
Even in the unlikely event of a breach, there is no actionable information at risk. Only you use our services,
not the other way around.

During the authentication process, your service will contact the following endpoints to verify the token if it signed by us.

First, it will contact the following endpoint to find the url of the `jwks.json` endpoint.
The jwks endpoint holds the public keys that are used to verify the token signature.

```http
https://<your-custom-domain-or-project-url>/.well-known/openid-configuration
```

Next, it will contact the following endpoint to get the public keys.

```http
https://<your-custom-domain-or-project-url>/.well-knonw/jwks.json
```

Requests to these endpoints are not logged.
