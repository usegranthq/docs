All endpoints are served from `https://sdk.usegrant.dev` and authenticated with a Bearer token, see [API Authentication](/core/auth).
On a self-hosted instance, the same endpoints are served under `/api/sdk` on your app's domain.

The OpenAPI specification is available at [`https://sdk.usegrant.dev/open-api.json`](https://sdk.usegrant.dev/open-api.json).
